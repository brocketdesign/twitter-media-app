How to download images and videos from a Twitter/X account

Paste a username, an @handle, or a full profile URL such as https://x.com/nasa into the box above and press Scan. The scanner walks that account's media timeline and lists everything on it — every photo and every video, including media the account reposted from others (marked "from @handle") — so you can look through it all on one page instead of scrolling the timeline. Nothing is uploaded anywhere: the files stream from X straight to your machine.

What you can do with the results

Download the images
Images land in their own tab as thumbnails with the date and the tweet text. Each one has a download button that saves the full-size original, not the compressed preview, and a link back to the tweet it came from.
Download the videos
Videos get a separate tab, each with the still frame X generated for it and a duration badge. Click to play inline before you commit to downloading, then grab the MP4 at the highest quality available.
Select images and videos
Tick the items you actually want. Selections work across both tabs at once, so you can mix photos and clips in a single batch, and they survive a page reload — leave and come back without picking again.
Download everything as a ZIP file
Download tab as ZIP bundles the whole tab into an archive so you don't click through items one at a time. The ZIP streams as it builds, and when a batch is too big for one archive it is split into numbered parts that download one after another.
Download everything from an account
Raise the scan limit to sweep up to 1000 media items in one pass, then ZIP each tab to take the account's full media history home — large tabs arrive as a series of smaller ZIPs.

Why does it ask for login cookies?

X/Twitter stopped serving media timelines to logged-out visitors, so a scan has to be made as a signed-in user. Paste the cookies from your own x.com session into the Login cookies box. They stay in your browser's local storage, are sent only to run your scan, and are never shared with anyone else.
